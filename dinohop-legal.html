Dino Hop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Developer: Raven Tobias

In short: Dino Hop does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. Your scores, coins and settings stay on your own device.

This policy explains what the mobile game Dino Hop (“the app”), made by Raven Tobias, does with information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not request any Android permissions.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, using Android’s local app storage:

  • your best score and coin total
  • the dinos you have unlocked and the one you selected
  • the time you last collected the free gift
  • your settings (vibration, sound and music)

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

4. Third-party services

The app contains no third-party services or SDKs that collect data. When you download the app, Google Play may collect information under Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and in the app with a new date.
